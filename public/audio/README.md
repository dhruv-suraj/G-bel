# Audio Files for Voice Demo

Place your pre-recorded audio files in this directory with the following names:

- **current-issues.mp3** - Audio explaining the current issues in the industry
- **use-cases.mp3** - Audio describing your use cases
- **what-we-provide.mp3** - Audio explaining what you provide

## Supported formats:
- MP3 (recommended)
- WAV
- OGG

## Notes:
- Audio files will play when users click the respective buttons in the Voice Demo modal
- Only one audio can play at a time (clicking another button will stop the current audio)
- Audio will stop automatically when the modal is closed
